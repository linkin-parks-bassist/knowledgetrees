Before deleting an ingested source, walk each substantive source section and map
every reusable claim to a leaf that directly contains its answer. Check details, not
merely headings or summaries. Unmapped claims must be distilled, deliberately marked
obsolete with a reason, or identified as non-knowledge. Then verify the affected tree
branches and repair every reference that would point to the deleted source.

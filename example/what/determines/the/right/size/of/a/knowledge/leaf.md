There is no context-free right leaf size. A reasoning agent should weigh semantic
relation, likely discovery paths, expected joint use, lifecycle, and retrieval cost.
A broader leaf can load irrelevant context; a fragmented answer can cost traversal,
tool calls, duplicated metadata, drift, and reconstruction. Those are considerations,
not a formula or ruling. Whether to preserve, split, or combine leaves remains a
contextual maintenance judgment.

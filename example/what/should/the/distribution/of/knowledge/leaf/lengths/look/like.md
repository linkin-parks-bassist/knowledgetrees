Expect a heavy-tailed length distribution: many short factual leaves, fewer medium
procedural or explanatory leaves, and a small number of substantial cohesive leaves.
An approximate inverse-rank `1/n` shape, or a stronger decay, is a useful intuition.
Do not enforce it as a quota; semantic cohesion and measured retrieval cost decide
individual boundaries.

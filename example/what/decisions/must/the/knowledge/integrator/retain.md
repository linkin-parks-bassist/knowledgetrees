The knowledge integrator retains responsibility for corpus coverage, semantic branch
shape, leaf atomicity, relevance, cross-leaf coherence, authority conflicts,
deprecation, portability, and the decision to retire source material. A worker may
extract or verify a crumb, but its output is evidence to review—not automatic
authority over the tree.

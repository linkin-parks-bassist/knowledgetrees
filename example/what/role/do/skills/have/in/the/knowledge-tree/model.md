Skills are a transitional compatibility bootstrap, not the target knowledge
architecture. The `knowledgetrees` skill teaches current agent harnesses to enter and
maintain the semantic tree; reusable knowledge itself belongs in atomic tree leaves.
Do not proliferate subject-matter skills or use skills as parallel monolithic
knowledge stores when the information can be discovered through the tree.

Tree poisoning is damage that pushes the maintained frontier away from current knowledge and makes later retrieval produce misleading or deranging guidance. The tree remembers what is true, not what happened; Git owns chronology.

The two canonical forms are:

- Tree inconsistency: active leaves give incompatible answers, or an active leaf contradicts checked source evidence. Stop relying on the affected knowledge and reconcile every owner immediately.
- Logleaves: a leaf accumulates chronology, session notes, progress narration, tool transcripts, or stream of consciousness instead of directly answering its path. Stale events then masquerade as current truth. Recompute the current answer; treat the old body as material to reconsider, not a template to extend. Keep a past fact only when it answers a current question or explains a present constraint or decision.

A green status or passing proof does not make poisoned prose safe. Prevention is mandatory: one current owner per answer, whole-leaf reconsideration before rewrite, immediate repair of contradictions, and no append-only knowledge logs. `kt audit` flags answer bodies over 1,000 words as suspect and date-shaped bodies as quite-suspect; those are inspection signals, not diagnoses or lifecycle states.

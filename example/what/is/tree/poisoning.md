Tree poisoning is damage to the maintained knowledge substrate that makes later retrieval produce misleading or deranging guidance. Some maintenance mistakes reliably drive agents into confused, contradictory, repetitive work and must be avoided at all costs.

The two canonical forms are:

- Tree inconsistency: active leaves give incompatible answers, or an active leaf contradicts checked source evidence. Stop relying on the affected knowledge and reconcile every owner immediately.
- Logleaves: a leaf accumulates chronology, session notes, progress narration, tool transcripts, or stream of consciousness instead of directly answering its path. Stale events then masquerade as current truth and poison retrieval. Rewrite the current answer in place; keep only history that explains a present constraint or decision, and put chronology in Git or a purpose-built external log.

A green status or passing proof does not make poisoned prose safe. Prevention is mandatory: one current owner per answer, whole-leaf review before rewrite, immediate repair of contradictions, and no append-only knowledge logs.

Source content is reusable knowledge when a future agent could need it to understand
requirements, make a decision, perform a procedure, use an interface, diagnose a
failure, assess current state, or verify acceptance. Task chronology, rhetorical
padding, duplicated explanation, and superseded instructions are not preserved as
answers unless their history itself explains a current constraint.

A leaf is never a log. Session notes, progress narration, tool transcripts, and
append-only chronology are tree poisoning: they mingle stale events with current
answers and degrade later retrieval. Rewrite current truth in its owner; retain
chronology in Git or a purpose-built external log.

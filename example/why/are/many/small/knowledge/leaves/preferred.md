Many small leaves are common because focused retrieval reduces irrelevant context,
repeated research, and missed answers. They are preferred only while that benefit
exceeds the real cost of more files: wider branches, filesystem and tool calls, Git
noise, repeated metadata, cross-link maintenance, gardening, and drift. Atomicity is
a bias, not a hard invariant; consolidate related answers when fragmentation makes the
tree less efficient.
